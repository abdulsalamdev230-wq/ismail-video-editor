PUT YOUR PROFILE PHOTO HERE
===========================

File name:  profile.png        (or keep .png and save it as-is)

Recommended:
- Professional headshot / upper-body portrait
- Roughly 1000 x 1220 px (a ~1 : 1.2 portrait ratio)
- PNG or JPG (JPG is smaller; just keep the name "profile.png" in data.js or update js/data.js)

When this file exists, the website automatically shows your photo in:
  1. the HERO panel (right side, with cinematic purple lighting)
  2. the ABOUT section (same treatment)
  3. the small avatar next to the hero eyebrow

Until you add it, visitors simply see the labelled placeholder — no fake photo is ever shown.

Your thumbnails and covers can live in this folder too:
  images/my-video-cover.jpg   →  set thumbnail: "images/my-video-cover.jpg" in js/data.js
